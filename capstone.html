Senior Design Project

A two-semester capstone project aimed at solving a real-world problem, with a tight deadline.

Project Overview

In the beginning of the project, I held the Documentation and Risk Manager role, which meant I was responsible for assessing the risk of our proposed solutions for both the Sponsor, and the team.


In the second semester of the project, I took on the role of Documentation and Training Manager, which meant I was responsible for creating training materials for the Sponsor to use when they were ready to implement our solution. I took the lead with communicating with the client and ensuring that they understood the product and how to use it.


In the final presentation of the project, I was in charge of the demo of the three solutions we created. Not only did I showcase all aspects of the solutions to the panel of judges, but I also kept the team within the time constraint of the presentation.


We were the best team in the class and were selected to present our solutions at the Capstone Competition, where the top 5 capstone projects were showcased, which was a great honor.
