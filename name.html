About me

Built for clean code, good vibes, and loud fandom energy.

I am silly, fun, outgoing, and very nerdy on purpose. This page is the short version of how I show up as a teammate, builder, and person.

What people notice first

I like making things that feel alive instead of sterile. If a portfolio, app, or project can be useful and memorable, I will usually push it in that direction.

My taste tends to lean playful, but my standards are serious: readable UI, thoughtful layout, accessible interactions, and code that makes maintenance easier later.

How I work

Default mode Curious, organized, and a little over invested in the details.
Favorite pattern Build, test, clean up, then make it look intentional.
Best strength Turning a vague idea into something polished and shippable.
